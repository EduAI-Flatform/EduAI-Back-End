# GDS card service-limit contract foundation

Owner scope: service quotas first; no new course grants. Default card tiers remain extensible through Admin. Annual spend/top-up qualification and quota reset periods are separate concepts.

Membership policies may now include optional `serviceLimits: { enabled, consumptionPriority, termAnchorAt, termMonths }` and per-tier `serviceLimits: [{ definitionId, quota }]`. Existing service definitions remain authoritative in the upcoming provisioning integration. This slice validates reference syntax/uniqueness and PostgreSQL-bigint-safe positive quota strings; it does not yet verify database definition existence/type or grant any usage. Explicit zero means no positive quota and is rejected; omit a service rule to give that tier no card quota.

Legacy revisions without these fields remain valid and unchanged. Validation fills defaults in a copy only; stored JSON, effective policy content and default public configuration are not rewritten. Unknown properties and client-supplied remaining balances/provenance remain rejected. Default limits are disabled, with no inferred fixed-term anchor or duration.

The pure period resolver supports lifetime, UTC calendar-month and explicitly anchored calendar-month terms, independently of the rolling rank window. Terms clamp from the original anchor at month end/leap years without cumulative drift, preserve explicit timezone offsets and use an exclusive upper boundary. Future/unconfigured/unknown periods fail closed.

Card impact adds `projectedServiceLimitChangesOnPage`, per-user `previousServiceLimits` and `projectedServiceLimitsChanged`. Its `serviceLimitImpactScope: configuration_only` and `serviceConsumptionProjected: false` explicitly distinguish configured-rule comparison from the future consumed-quota projection. Disabled rules have no executable impact. Existing descriptive-benefit/tier counts and preservation flags remain intact.

Until the provenance migration and atomic provisioning/consumption integration are implemented and verified, enabling executable limits is blocked by `CARD_SERVICE_LIMIT_RUNTIME_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` in both preview and schedule. Drafts and disabled configurations remain usable; ordinary card classification and independently purchased rights are unchanged. This is a staging boundary to remove/replace with verified runtime readiness in the next slice, not a permanent completion claim.

Validation: new typed draft acceptance and impact/activation regressions failed before implementation. The activation test initially used the wrong Date argument/exception expectation, then was corrected against the existing scheduler contract; its exact blocker payload and zero-update assertion are retained. All 97 related tests across 10 card/platform/request suites and Nest build passed. Coverage includes maximum numeric range rejection, duplicate references, extra rule keys, invalid/future/missing anchors, leap/end-of-month/offset boundaries, legacy-content preservation and independent quota/rank configuration. No dependency, schema, balance, grant or financial activation change is included. Required PR/main CI, release and bounded draft acceptance must be recorded separately.
